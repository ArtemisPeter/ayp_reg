# AYP Registration

Registration portal for the annual Youth Camp of the South Mindanao District of CAMACOP in the Philippines. It replaces manual registration for roughly 6,000 attendees.

## Language

**CAMACOP**:
The international Christian denomination this system serves. Only its Philippine operations are in scope.

**District**:
A regional division of CAMACOP in the Philippines. This system serves one: the South Mindanao District (SMD).

**Circuit**:
A division of a District made up of one municipality or city, or several merged together. Circuits compete against each other in camp contests.
_Avoid_: Municipality, city, chapter

**Youth**:
A member of the Young People group of the church: teenagers up to age 40 who are unmarried. The church's other groups are Children, Men and Women, the last two being married adults.
_Avoid_: Young People (use in prose only when naming the church group)

**Pastor**:
A minister of CAMACOP who attends the Youth Camp. Pastors are also organisers of the camp.

**Attendee**:
A Youth or a Pastor who is registered for a Youth Camp and receives a Camp ID.
_Avoid_: Delegate, participant, registrant, camper

**Camp ID**:
The physical, printable ID card of an Attendee, sized a quarter of an A4 sheet. The front shows only the Attendee's first name and Circuit, in the Circuit's colour, and the back carries a QR code.
_Avoid_: Badge, pass, ID card

**Circuit Colour**:
The one colour assigned to each Circuit, used on its Camp IDs.

**Visitor**:
Anyone at the Youth Camp who is neither a Youth nor a Pastor. Visitors are never registered and get no Camp ID.
_Avoid_: Guest

**Baptismal**:
A yes-or-no record of whether an Attendee has been baptised. It is recorded only and never affects registration.

**Local Church**:
A single church belonging to a Circuit. Every registrant belongs to one.
_Avoid_: Branch, congregation

**Local Church President**:
The leader of a Local Church, who prepares that church's list of attendees.

**Circuit President**:
The leader of a Circuit, who receives Local Church lists, confirms payment outside the system, and submits the list to the system.

**Secretary**:
A District staff user who can view, add, edit and delete every Attendee's information.

**Security**:
A gate user who can only scan an Attendee's QR code and see their Name, Local Church and Circuit.
_Avoid_: Guard, gate keeper

**Gate Scan**:
A record that Security scanned an Attendee's Camp ID as going IN or OUT of the camp. Security chooses the direction before scanning; the system does not infer it.
_Avoid_: Check-in, attendance, log

**Tabulator**:
A user who enters the contest events and scores, from which the Circuit leaderboard is computed.
_Avoid_: Scorer, judge

**Admin**:
A user with unrestricted control of the system, including creating other users.
_Avoid_: Superadmin

**Event**:
A contest held during a Youth Camp in which Circuits compete, such as Basketball or Bible Quiz. Each Event has its own points for each Rank.
_Avoid_: Contest, competition, game

**Rank**:
A Circuit's placing in an Event, such as Champion, or L for a Circuit that lost.

**Leaderboard**:
The standing of Circuits, ordered by the total points they earned across all Events of a Youth Camp. Circuits with equal totals are listed alphabetically and marked TIE.
_Avoid_: Ranking, scoreboard

**Youth Camp**:
The annual December gathering for the District's young people, held once per year. Each year has its own theme.
_Avoid_: Conference, camp meeting

**Pre-Registration**:
Registration completed before the Youth Camp, submitted by a Circuit President after payment is confirmed.
_Avoid_: Pre-reg, advance registration

**On-Site Registration**:
Registration completed at the Youth Camp itself, capturing the same information as Pre-Registration.
_Avoid_: Walk-in, late registration
